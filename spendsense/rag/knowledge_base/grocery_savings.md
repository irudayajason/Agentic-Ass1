# Spending Smarter on Groceries

Groceries are a need, but how you buy them changes the bill a lot.

## Quick-commerce vs planned shopping
10-minute delivery apps (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BigBasket) are convenient but encourage many small top-up orders, each with fees and impulse add-ons. One planned weekly or monthly shop at a supermarket or wholesale store, with a list, usually costs less per item.

## Habits that lower the bill
- Plan meals for the week and buy only what the plan needs.
- Buy staples (rice, dal, oil, atta) in bulk once a month.
- Compare the price per kg or litre instead of the pack price.
- Buy fruit and vegetables from local markets, in season.
- Check the fridge and pantry before ordering, to avoid waste.

## A useful signal
If you see more than 8-10 quick-commerce orders a month, combine them into fewer planned orders. Saving ₹100-₹150 per order is common.
