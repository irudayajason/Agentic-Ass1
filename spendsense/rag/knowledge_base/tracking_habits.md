# Tracking Spending: Habits That Stick

You can't manage what you don't look at. Tracking doesn't need to be daily or perfect.

## A weekly 10-minute review
Every Sunday, look at the week's transactions: total spent, the top three categories, and anything that surprised you. Compare with the same week last month.

## Methods that help
- **Envelope method**: give each wants category a fixed monthly amount; when it's used up, stop spending in that category until next month.
- **Category caps**: set limits only for the 2-3 categories that usually overshoot.
- **Separate accounts**: one for bills and fixed costs, one for daily spending. It makes overspending visible.

## Look for trends, not single days
Month-over-month change by category is more useful than any one transaction. A category rising more than 30% from one month to the next deserves a closer look.
