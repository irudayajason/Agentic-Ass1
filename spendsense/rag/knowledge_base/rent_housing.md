# Rent and Housing Costs

Rent is usually the single largest expense, so even a small change makes a big difference.

## A guideline
Try to keep rent at or below about 30% of take-home income. In expensive cities it may go higher; if so, keep wants spending tighter to protect savings.

## Ways to reduce housing cost
- Share a flat. It can cut rent and utilities by 30-50%.
- Weigh a slightly longer commute against much lower rent; include the extra travel cost in the comparison.
- Negotiate at renewal: offer a longer lease in exchange for a smaller increase.
- Keep rent receipts and the landlord's details; they may be useful for tax purposes such as HRA claims.

## Paying rent by UPI
Rent paid to a person by UPI looks like an ordinary transfer in a statement. Adding a note such as "RENT JUL" keeps your records clear.
