# Controlling Impulse Shopping and Sale-Season Spending

Online sales on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and similar sites, especially festival sales, are designed to create urgency: countdown timers, "only 2 left" and bank-card discounts.

## Simple rules that work
- **The 48-hour rule**: put non-essential items in the cart and wait two days. Many no longer feel necessary.
- **Make a list before a sale** and buy only from it. A discount on something you didn't need is not a saving.
- **Set a sale budget** in rupees before the sale starts.
- Unsubscribe from shopping emails and turn off app notifications.
- Avoid "Buy Now Pay Later" and no-cost EMI for wants; they make overspending painless now and expensive later.

## After a big shopping month
Check return windows for things you regret. Then plan a lower-spending month to rebuild savings, and set up a sinking fund (see festival expenses) so next year's sale is paid for in advance.
