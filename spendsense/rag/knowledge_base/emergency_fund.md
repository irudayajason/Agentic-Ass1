# Building an Emergency Fund

An emergency fund is money kept aside for job loss, medical bills or urgent repairs, so you don't have to borrow at high interest or sell investments at a bad time.

## How much to keep
A common guideline is **3 to 6 months of essential expenses** (needs, not total spending). Single earners, freelancers and people with dependants should aim for the higher end. Calculate it from your statement: average monthly spending on rent, groceries, bills, EMIs and transport, times six.

## Where to keep it
It should be safe and quick to access: a separate savings account or a sweep-in fixed deposit. It is not meant to earn high returns, so it should not sit in stocks.

## Building it step by step
Start with a first target of one month's expenses. Set up an automatic transfer on salary day, even ₹2,000-₹5,000. Put windfalls such as bonuses and refunds toward it until the target is reached. Refill it after you use it.
