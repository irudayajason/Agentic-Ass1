# Cutting Food Delivery and Eating-Out Costs

Food delivery apps such as Swiggy and Zomato, and eating out at restaurants and cafes, are among the fastest-growing spending categories for young earners in Indian cities. Each order feels small, but 15-30 orders a month add up quickly.

## Why delivery costs more than it looks
The menu prices on apps are often higher than in the restaurant, and delivery fees, platform fees, packaging charges and tips are added on top. An order shown as ₹350 commonly ends up costing ₹420-₹480.

## Practical ways to spend less
- Set a monthly cap (for example, 8 orders or a fixed rupee amount) and track it weekly.
- Cook in bulk on weekends, so weekday dinners need no ordering.
- Order for pickup or eat at the restaurant to skip delivery fees.
- Remove saved cards and turn off promotional notifications. Fewer prompts lead to fewer impulse orders.
- Replace late-night snack orders with snacks bought at the supermarket.

## Estimating the saving
Cutting 10 orders of about ₹400 each saves about ₹4,000 a month, or ₹48,000 a year.
