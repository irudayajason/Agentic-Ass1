# Handling Bonuses, Raises and Windfalls

Extra money is the easiest money to save, because your lifestyle doesn't depend on it yet.

## Avoid lifestyle creep
When income rises, spending tends to rise to match. Decide in advance to save at least half of any raise, for example by raising your SIP or automatic transfer the same month.

## A simple split for one-time money
For a bonus, tax refund or gift: first fill any gap in the emergency fund, then prepay high-interest debt, then invest toward goals, and keep a small part (for example 10-20%) to enjoy.

## Refunds and cashback
Treat refunds as money returned to your budget, not as free money to spend again.
