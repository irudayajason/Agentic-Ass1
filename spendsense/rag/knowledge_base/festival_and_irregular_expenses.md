# Planning for Festivals and Irregular Expenses

Diwali, Pongal, weddings, birthdays, annual insurance premiums and yearly subscriptions don't come every month, so they often wreck the month they land in.

## Sinking funds
Estimate the yearly cost of each irregular expense and divide it by 12. Move that amount into a separate account every month. For example, ₹24,000 of festival spending becomes ₹2,000 a month.

## Before festival season
- Set a total festival budget covering gifts, clothes, sweets, travel and home items.
- Use the festival sale for planned purchases only (see impulse shopping).
- Plan gifts early to avoid last-minute premium prices.

## Bonuses
If you receive a festival or performance bonus, decide its split before it arrives. For example, some to the emergency fund or loan prepayment, some to investments, and some to enjoy guilt-free.
