# Saving on Daily Transport

Cab and auto rides (Uber, Ola, Rapido), metro and bus fares, fuel and parking can quietly become a large monthly cost.

## Ideas by mode
- **Ride-hailing**: surge pricing at peak hours can double fares. Leaving 15 minutes earlier, sharing rides or switching to bike-taxis for short trips cuts costs.
- **Public transport**: a metro or bus pass is usually far cheaper per trip than cabs for a regular commute.
- **Own vehicle**: keep tyres at the right pressure, service it on time, and combine errands into one trip. Track fuel spend per month.
- **Parking**: monthly passes near the office are cheaper than paying per visit.

## A quick test
Divide your monthly transport spend by the number of working days. If daily commuting costs more than about 5-8% of your daily take-home pay, look for a cheaper mix of modes.
