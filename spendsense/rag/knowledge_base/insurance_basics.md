# Insurance Basics

Insurance protects savings from large shocks. It is a cost of protection, not an investment.

## Health insurance
A hospital stay can cost lakhs. An individual or family floater health policy, separate from any employer cover that ends when you change jobs, is a key safety net.

## Term life insurance
If anyone depends on your income, pure term insurance gives a large cover for a relatively low premium. Policies that mix insurance with investment usually offer less cover and lower returns than keeping the two separate.

## Budgeting premiums
Annual premiums are irregular expenses: divide them by 12 and save monthly in a sinking fund. In spending analysis, treat premiums as needs.
