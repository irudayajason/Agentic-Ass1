# Money Sent to Friends and Family

Person-to-person UPI transfers include splitting bills, lending money, gifts and supporting family. In a statement they look alike, which makes them easy to overlook.

## Splits and lending
- Settle shared expenses quickly, using a bill-splitting app or a running note, so money owed to you isn't forgotten.
- Lend only what you could afford never to get back, and agree on a repayment date.
- Compare money sent with money received from the same people. A big gap can mean unpaid dues.

## Supporting family
Regular support to parents or siblings is a real commitment, not a "want". Budget it as a fixed monthly expense so it doesn't feel like a leak.
