# Auditing Subscriptions and AutoPay Mandates

OTT streaming (Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar, YouTube Premium), music (Spotify), cloud storage, gym and app subscriptions renew silently through UPI AutoPay or card mandates. "Subscription creep" means adding new ones without cancelling old ones.

## How to find them
Look for the same merchant charged the same amount every month, or descriptions with "AutoPay", "mandate" or "SI". List every one with its monthly cost and the last time you actually used it.

## What to do
- Cancel anything you haven't used in the last 30 days.
- Keep one or two streaming services and rotate them every few months instead of paying for all at once.
- Switch to annual plans only for services you use daily; they are cheaper per month but lock money in.
- Check family or shared plans for music and storage.
- Review mandates in your UPI app under AutoPay settings and revoke the ones you no longer need.

## Why it matters
Three forgotten ₹150-₹650 subscriptions can cost ₹10,000-₹15,000 a year.
