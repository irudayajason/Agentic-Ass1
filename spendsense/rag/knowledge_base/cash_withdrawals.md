# Cash Withdrawals and Untracked Spending

ATM withdrawals show up in a statement only as "cash", so whatever the cash is spent on disappears from your tracking.

## Why it matters
If cash withdrawals are a significant share of spending, your category totals understate real spending in food, transport or shopping. Several withdrawals a month can also attract ATM charges after the free limit.

## Tips
- Withdraw a fixed amount for the week and note what it's for.
- Prefer UPI or card for most payments so spending is tracked automatically.
- Use your own bank's ATMs and stay within the free transactions each month.
