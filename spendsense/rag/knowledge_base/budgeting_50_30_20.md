# The 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule

A simple way to split take-home income into three buckets. It is a starting point, not a law; adjust it to your city and stage of life.

## The three buckets
- **Needs (50%)**: rent, groceries, electricity, mobile and internet, commuting, insurance premiums and minimum loan EMIs. Things you must pay to live and work.
- **Wants (30%)**: eating out and food delivery, shopping, entertainment, subscriptions, trips and gadgets.
- **Savings (20%)**: emergency fund, SIPs and other investments, extra loan prepayments.

## How to apply it to a bank statement
Add up each month's debits by category and compare each bucket with the percentages above. Investments such as a SIP count as savings even though they appear as debits. If wants are above 30% and savings below 20%, the fastest fix is usually in the wants bucket.

## When the rule does not fit
In metro cities rent alone can take 30-40% of income. Then keep wants tighter (around 20%) so savings stay close to 20%. Early in a career, even a 10% savings rate that you keep up every month beats an ambitious target you abandon.
