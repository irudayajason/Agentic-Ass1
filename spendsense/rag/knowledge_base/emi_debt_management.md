# Managing EMIs and Debt

Loans are useful for big needs, but EMIs reduce how much you can save every month for years.

## How much EMI is safe
A common guideline is to keep **total EMIs below 30-40% of take-home income**. Above that, any surprise expense can push you into more borrowing.

## Priorities
1. Never miss an EMI or a credit card due date. Late fees and credit score damage are expensive.
2. Pay off the highest-interest debt first (credit card dues and personal loans before vehicle or home loans).
3. Pay credit card bills in full. Paying only the "minimum due" means very high interest on the rest.
4. Use bonuses to prepay costly loans, after checking for prepayment charges.

## Watch out for
"No-cost EMI" and Buy Now Pay Later for small purchases. Many small EMIs add up and hide the true monthly cost.
