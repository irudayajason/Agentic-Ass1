# Staying Safe with UPI Payments

Fraud can drain savings faster than any overspending.

## Key rules
- You never need to enter your UPI PIN to **receive** money. A request asking for your PIN to "receive" a refund or prize is a scam.
- Check the payee name shown before confirming any payment.
- Never share OTPs, PINs or screen-sharing access with anyone claiming to be from a bank or app.
- Decline unknown "collect" requests.
- Review your statement for transactions you don't recognise, and report fraud quickly to your bank and the national cybercrime helpline (1930).

## Limits
Set a sensible daily UPI limit in your bank app, and revoke AutoPay mandates you no longer use.
