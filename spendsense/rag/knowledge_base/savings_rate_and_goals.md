# Savings Rate and Paying Yourself First

Your savings rate is the share of income you keep: (income - spending) / income. It is the single best number for tracking financial health.

## Target ranges
- Below 10%: fragile; one bad month can wipe out savings.
- 10-20%: reasonable starting range.
- 20% or more: healthy, in line with the 50/30/20 rule.
Count investments such as SIPs as savings when you calculate it.

## Pay yourself first
Move savings out on salary day, by automatic transfer or SIP, before you spend on anything else. Then spend what is left. This works better than trying to save whatever remains at the end of the month.

## Set goals with numbers and dates
"Save more" is vague. "Save ₹60,000 for an emergency fund by June, which is ₹7,500 a month" is something you can track.
