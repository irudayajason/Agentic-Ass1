# Investing Basics (General Education)

This note explains general ideas only. It is not personalised investment advice; for decisions about specific products, consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser.

## Before you invest
Build an emergency fund and clear high-interest debt first. Investing while carrying credit card debt rarely makes sense.

## Key ideas
- **Compounding**: returns earn further returns over time, so starting early matters more than starting big.
- **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)**: investing a fixed amount every month, usually in mutual funds. It builds discipline and averages out market ups and downs.
- **Diversification**: spreading money across asset types reduces the impact of any one going badly.
- **Match the product to the goal**: money needed within 1-3 years should not be in volatile assets like equities.
- **Costs matter**: expense ratios and fees reduce returns every year.

## A healthy habit
Treat your SIP as a fixed "bill" paid on salary day, and increase it when your income rises.
