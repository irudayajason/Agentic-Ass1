# Lowering Bills and Utilities

Bills are needs, but most can be trimmed without hurting comfort.

## Electricity
Air-conditioning is the biggest summer driver. Setting the AC to 24-26°C, using a fan alongside it, cleaning filters, and switching to LED lighting and star-rated appliances all reduce bills. Expect higher bills in April-August and budget for it.

## Mobile and internet
Compare your actual data use with your plan. Many people pay for far more data than they use, or pay separately for mobile data and home broadband when Wi-Fi covers most of their day. Long-validity prepaid plans are often cheaper per month.

## Cooking gas and water
Use pressure cookers and lids, fix leaking taps, and book cylinders before you run out to avoid buying at higher prices.

## Pay on time
Late fees and reconnection charges are pure waste. Use auto-pay or calendar reminders for due dates.
