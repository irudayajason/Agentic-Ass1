# The Hidden Cost of Small UPI Payments

UPI made paying ₹20 for tea or ₹40 for parking effortless. Because each payment is tiny, people rarely notice how many they make.

## The pattern
Thirty to fifty payments under ₹200 a month is common. Individually they are harmless; together they can reach ₹3,000-₹6,000 a month, more than many people's electricity bill.

## How to manage it
- Look at your count of small payments, not only the amount. A rising count is an early warning.
- Set a weekly "pocket money" limit for small spends (for example ₹700 a week).
- Some people move a fixed amount to a separate wallet or UPI Lite balance and spend small payments only from it. When it's empty, the week's small spending is done.
- Make tea, coffee and snacks at home or at the office on most days.

## Keep perspective
Small treats are fine. The goal is to choose them on purpose, not to cut every chai.
